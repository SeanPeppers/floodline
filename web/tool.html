    Inputs, method and limits
    • Each road point is the street's centreline elevation (USGS 3DEP lidar) every ~30 m. Gutters sit lower, so real ponding starts earlier.
    • The rule is multidriver.py's: water from the sea, groundwater 0.5–1.5 ft above high tide (City of Key West 2024 Stormwater Master Plan) and rain the drains can't take; a street floods past 6 inches of water.
    • Absolute probabilities depend on the drainage calibration, an assumption: the five-driver and two-driver calibrations disagree at Patterson Ave (about 0.97 vs 0.64 a year).
    • Simplified: no waves, wind setup, tidal lag in groundwater, or blocked drains. Rain spread ×0.5–×2 is our assumption, not an NWS figure.
    • Data: NOAA CO-OPS and NWS api.weather.gov, fetched by your browser, no account and no data about you sent. Roads © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL).
