What we measured, how, and where it breaks.

We estimate a Key West street’s annual flood probability and count the model queries each method needs to hit a target error ε. On ideal hardware, maximum-likelihood QAE needs 4.3× [3.5, 5.4] fewer queries than the best classical method we found (conditional MC on Sobol points) at 13 uncertain factors, ε = 1e-4. With the 5 physical drivers alone, it loses.

  • Query counts only. Wall-clock time and today’s noisy hardware both favour classical.
  • Every ratio carries a 95% bootstrap interval; an interval containing 1 is a tie.

3On a real quantum computer

The hardware follows the Grover oscillation that QAE relies on, and noise damps it as circuits deepen (12 to 192 two-qubit gates); the noisy estimate is 0.074 vs exact 0.096, which is why today's hardware can't beat classical.

4Verdict: wins from 9 factors, in query count. Loses at 5.

Shown

  • 4.3× fewer queries than CMC+QMC at 13 factors, 3.0× at 9–10 (ε = 1e-4, ideal hardware).
  • Quadratic edge over plain MC: α = 0.91 vs 2.01.
  • QAE passes Sobol QMC near 4 drivers.
  • A working street and city flood tool.

Not shown

  • A win at 5 factors (0.65×), or at ε = 1e-3 at any d.
  • A circuit for the 8 extra parameters.
  • Hardware speedup. On IBM ibm_fez the 3-qubit demo estimates 0.074 vs exact 0.096; the full street circuit turns into a coin flip (0.50 vs ideal 0.64, noisy simulation).
  • Wall-clock gains: a quantum query costs far more than a sample.
  • A calibrated forecast.

5Methods and assumptions

Two-driver street model

Tide: 110 annual maxima from NOAA gauge 8724580, shifted to 2025 sea level, GEV fit. Rain: NOAA Atlas 14 24-hour depths, GEV fit. The Patterson Ave rule uses the Master Plan's curve number and tide runs. Annual flood chance: 0.640.

Tide and rain share a Gaussian copula with τ = 0.3, our assumption. Wahl et al. (2015) report significant surge–rain dependence at virtually all Gulf and east-coast sites; Key West appears only in their Fig. 1b, and they used daily surge and rain, not annual maxima.

Also assumed: linear storage, square-root drainage, and annual tide and rain peaks counted as one event.

Five-driver model

Adds a 3-hour burst, NOAA 2022 sea-level rise and groundwater (Master Plan range). Assumed: drainage calibration, catchment ratio, tide–rain τ = 0.2, burst ρ = 0.8. The circuit matches the closed form at 20 qubits; full-scale QAE uses the ideal law. See README_multidriver.md.

Estimators and hardware

QAE is maximum-likelihood amplitude estimation. One query is one circuit call or one classical sample. Exponents and ratios carry 95% bootstrap intervals; an interval containing 1 is a tie.

The deepest 5-driver circuit, Q256A, has 7.6e7 CX gates on 52 logical qubits. Half-time success needs gate error near 9.1e-9; IBM Heron's snapshot median is 0.0039.

  • Floodline is a planning tool. It did not prevent, and doesn't claim to prevent, any of these losses.

Where the quantum result applies

Real flood models are black boxes.

  • ~630 SLOSH runs per NOAA P-Surge advisory, due within 1 hour
  • ~2,000 core-hours per ADCIRC storm-surge run
  • 1.6 / 2.3 / 3.3× fewer simulator runs for quantum than the best classical method, ε = 1e-3 / 3e-4 / 1e-4

Inside a black box, our best classical method has to bisect for each sample: 6 simulator runs. That is where QAE wins at five drivers.

How we calculated this

runs saved = N × (1 − 1/r), then $ = runs saved × CPU-hours per run × $0.0473. Low / central / high use r = 1.6 / 2.3 / 3.3, our measured black-box ratios at ε = 1e-3 / 3e-4 / 1e-4 (5 drivers, 3.0 ft street).

Inputs:

  • P-Surge: ~630 SLOSH runs per advisory, results due within an hour. NOAA SLOSH MPI paper (AMS 2020)
  • FEMA Louisiana study: 446 synthetic storms. Louisiana 2017 suite: 7,712 simulations, ~10M CPU-hours. Louisiana Coastal Master Plan slides
  • ADCIRC+SWAN: ~2,000 core-hours per run. arXiv 1909.01836
  • Hurricane Ike run: 1,064 cores × 2.683 h = 2,855 core-hours (FEMA high case). arXiv 2310.06199
  • Price: AWS hpc7a.96xlarge, $9.0793/h ÷ 192 vCPU = $0.0473 per CPU-hour (region unstated). Vantage

Assumptions:

  1. Our 5-driver ratios carry over to SLOSH and ADCIRC. Untested.
  2. The classical method finds each threshold by bisection. Smarter search shrinks the win.
  3. Fault-tolerant hardware about a million times cleaner than today's: two-qubit error 1e-8–1e-9 vs ~4e-3, with the simulator compiled to a reversible circuit.
  4. Quantum machine cost is not counted. It is likely larger than the savings.
  5. QAE queries run one after another; P-Surge's 630 runs run in parallel. QAE may lose on wall-clock time.
