Before we start

What you’re about to watch

  • A real storm: Hurricane Irma, a Category 4, hitting the Florida Keys on 10 Sep 2017.
  • Key West in 3-D: we drop down onto the island and raise the water through the official flood stages.
  • The question: how likely is a given street to flood? Answering it means running a flood model a huge number of times.
  • The payoff: a live race between the normal way (Monte Carlo) and a quantum algorithm, then the results.

Recap

What you just watched

  • Irma, 10 Sep 2017: a Category 4 landfall at Cudjoe Key, about 20 miles from Key West.
  • The surge: the Key West gauge peaked 2.7 ft above high tide, past NWS major flood stage.
  • The thresholds: minor stage (0.335 m) is reached in 96% of years; major stage (0.64 m), from tide alone, about 1 year in 22.
  • Next: watch Monte Carlo and quantum race to estimate those flood odds, then see where quantum beats the best classical method.
